Principles

1 · Show state, always. Loading, empty, error, success — every fetch has 4 states. Build all four.

2 · Optimistic by default. Local change first, server confirmation second. Revert with a toast on failure.

3 · Confirm only the destructive. Don't ask "are you sure?" for things that can be undone. Do ask for delete and pay.

4 · Keyboard-first navigation. Tab moves through every interactive element in reading order. Esc closes overlays.

5 · One primary action per surface. If you have two CTAs, one of them is wrong.

6 · Forgive small mistakes. Trim whitespace from emails. Auto-format phone numbers. Accept "tomorrow" as a date.

7 · Latency > 200ms shows feedback. Below that, instant feels right. Above, show a skeleton or spinner.
